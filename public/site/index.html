Native branching · first-class UI

Branch from a sentence, not just a message.

Something in an answer catches your eye. Select it and follow that thread in its own conversation, right beside the one you’re in.

  • Branch from any selection or any message
  • Branches stay nested in a tree next to the original chat
  • A gutter marker stays beside the passage you branched from
  • Hover to highlight the source text; click to reopen the branch

In most AI chats, a branch is a copy of the whole conversation, filed away as one more chat.

Your days sit on one continuous page. Scroll back to last Tuesday and see what you wrote, what was due, and what you talked through.

  • See every chat you had on or about that day
  • Notes you took inside a chat or a PDF still show up under their day

In most note apps, a daily page only knows what you typed on it.

  • Link notes, chats, documents and days inline with @
  • Focus on any node to work on one idea and its children, then step back out

  • A note shows every chat that came out of it

In most apps, your AI chats and your notes live in separate places.

Thinking

A thinking tool, not a dumbing-down device.

Hand over the thinking and you keep the answer, not the understanding. SideQuestly is arranged the other way round — your writing and your layout come first, and the AI works on what you put there.

Writing

Long-form writing, in a real outliner.

  • Headings, quotes, tables, code and images — in bullets you can fold, move and zoom into.
  • Zoom into a single bullet to work on one idea at a time, then step back out.
  • Answers don’t rewrite your page: what reaches your notes is what you capture, with a link back to where it came from.

In most AI chats, the only long piece of writing on the screen is the AI’s.

Canvas

Lay out how the ideas connect.

  • Cards are references, not copies — a note on a canvas is the same note as the one in your journal.
  • Draw arrows between cards and label them, so the relation is in your words.
  • A chat card reads exactly the cards you connected to it, using your labels as the relation.

Most AI tools hand back a finished structure. The arrangement is theirs, not yours.

Memory

Remember one sentence without adding a word to the chat.

  • Select a passage, say exactly what to keep, and save it. Nothing is added to the conversation.
  • Already have something similar? See both and keep them separate, update the old one, or replace it.
  • It saves only what you ask it to. Everything it notices waits for your OK, and replaced memories stay restorable.

Most AI chats save memories on their own as you talk. You can delete one, but not bring it back.

Context

See what an answer was built from, and what didn’t fit.

  • A ring shows how full the conversation is. Near the limit, trim older search results or branch into a fresh one.
  • Workspace instructions and notes shape every chat in that workspace.

Most AI chats never show how full a conversation is. Older parts quietly stop counting.
